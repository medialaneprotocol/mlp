# Code of conduct

## The standard

This project expects participants to be **considerate, respectful, and
collaborative**. A protocol is a contract between people who will not
meet, so the way disagreements are handled is part of the protocol's
quality.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith.
- Critique the **work**, never the person. "This rule is wrong because…"
  is the useful form; "you clearly did not understand…" is not.
- When you disagree, say what you think the rule should be and why. A
  disagreement without a proposal is a complaint.
- Accept that a maintainer may decide against your proposal. The
  discussion is the point; the decision is theirs to make.
- Assume good faith in the face of apparent bad faith, and say so plainly
  when it is not apparent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or any other conduct that would
  reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the organisation's administrators. A
report is handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not uphold this
policy in their own interactions are subject to the same consequences as
anyone else.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behaviour, and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action
in response to any instance of unacceptable behaviour.

## Scope

This applies in the project's repositories, in its issues and discussions,
and wherever someone is representing the project publicly.
